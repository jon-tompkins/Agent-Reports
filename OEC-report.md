## 1. Company Overview

### Business Description
Orion S.A. is a global manufacturer of carbon black products, headquartered in Senningerberg, Luxembourg. Founded in 1862, the company was formerly known as Orion Engineered Carbons S.A. until rebranding in June 2023.

### Business Segments
| Segment | Products | Applications |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Specialty Carbon Black** | Post-treated grades, high-purity grades, conductive grades | Coatings, printing, fiber industry, batteries, polymers |
| **Rubber Carbon Black** | High-reinforcing grades, semi-reinforcing grades (ECORAX brand) | Tires, automotive, construction, medical, consumer goods |

### Geographic Footprint
- **Americas:** United States, Brazil
- **EMEA:** Germany, South Africa, Italy, Spain, Czech Republic, Poland, Turkey, France
- **Asia-Pacific:** China, South Korea, and broader region
- 13+ production facilities globally

### Product Portfolio

- **Tires/Automotive:** Largest demand driver for rubber carbon black
