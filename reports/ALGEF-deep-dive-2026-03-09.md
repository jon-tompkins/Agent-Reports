## The Business

Alligator Energy Limited is an Australian uranium exploration and development company headquartered in Spring Hill, Australia. The company is focused on advancing uranium and energy-related mineral projects, with operations spanning Australia and Italy.

**Core Business Model:**
- **Primary Focus:** In-situ recovery (ISR) uranium development — the same low-cost extraction method used at Australia's only operating uranium mines
- **Revenue Stage:** Pre-production explorer; minimal revenue (~A$1.1M from investments/interest, not uranium sales)
- **Path to Production:** Targeting 2027 first production at flagship Samphire project

**Key Projects:**
| Project | Location | Stage | Resource |
|---------|----------|-------|----------|
| **Samphire** (Flagship) | South Australia | Development | 17.5Mlb U3O8 (12.9Mlb Indicated) |
| **Big Lake** | Cooper Basin, SA | Exploration | Greenfield discovery |
| **Piedmont** | Northern Italy | JV Partnership | Ni-Cu-Co (strategic optionality) |
| ~~Alligator Rivers~~ | Northern Territory | **Sold to DevEx** | Divested for $7.5M |

The company has streamlined its portfolio to focus entirely on Samphire — Australia's most advanced ISR uranium development project not yet in production.

## Bull Case 🐂

1. **Right place, right time:** Only jurisdiction in Australia with operating uranium mines; clear permitting pathway; supportive state government

2. **Experienced team:** 65 years of combined ISR experience from the actual Beverley/Four Mile operations — they literally wrote the playbook

3. **Macro tailwinds:** Nuclear renaissance driven by AI/data center demand, energy security concerns, and climate commitments; uranium prices at multi-year highs with structural deficit ahead

4. **Near-term de-risking:** Field recovery trial results in Q1-Q2 2026 can validate the deposit and unlock re-rating; BFS in early 2027

5. **Upside optionality:** Big Lake is a greenfield discovery in a largely unexplored basin — potential for second major deposit

6. **Balance sheet strength:** ~A$20M+ cash, no debt, funded through feasibility without immediate dilution

---

## Bear Case 🐻

1. **Execution risk:** Boss Energy's Honeymoon struggled with underperforming resources — ISR is not guaranteed to work as modeled. Field trial could disappoint.

2. **Pre-revenue reality:** Still 18-24+ months from production; sustained cash burn; will need more capital (likely $100M+) for construction

3. **Dilution history:** Share count up 45%+ since 2021 — more dilution coming when they raise for construction

4. **Uranium price dependency:** Economics work at $75+/lb but highly sensitive to spot price; sustained prices <$60/lb would stress the project

5. **Small resource:** 17.5Mlb is smaller than peers like Boss (71Mlb); limited mine life without exploration success at Big Lake

6. **OTC liquidity:** ALGEF is thinly traded on US OTC; most volume is on ASX (AGE.AX); price can gap on low volume

7. **Political risk:** While SA is uranium-friendly, Australian federal politics around nuclear remain contentious (nuclear power ban still in place)

## Scout's Take

"This is Australia's next uranium mine — if the trial works. The team is legit (ex-Beverley, not fintwit mining promoters), the jurisdiction is proven (literally the only place in Oz where you can do this), and the timing is perfect with uranium over $80/lb and heading into a structural deficit.

But it's still a trial. Boss Energy got burned when their deposit didn't perform to model. AGE's MD is explicitly building in rigor to avoid that — but until we see results, this is a show-me story.

The setup is: buy ahead of trial results (now), hold for BFS, potentially scale up into production if they nail it. Downside is they don't nail it, stock gives back the 2024 gains.

This is a speculative uranium development play with better-than-average odds. Worth a Jeb/Ant look if Jon wants uranium exposure in a name that isn't already at scale."
