## The Business

Oracle is a 48-year-old enterprise software company undergoing one of the most aggressive transformations in tech history—pivoting from database licensing to AI cloud infrastructure.

**How They Make Money (Q2 FY26 Revenue: $16.1B):**

| Segment | Revenue | % of Total | YoY Growth |
|---------|---------|------------|------------|
| Cloud Services | $8.0B | 50% | +34% |
| — Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) | $4.1B | 25% | +68% |
| — Cloud Applications (SaaS) | $3.9B | 25% | +11% |
| Software Licenses/Support | $5.9B | 37% | -3% |
| Services | $1.4B | 9% | +7% |
| Hardware | $0.8B | 5% | +7% |

**The pivot:** Cloud is now 50% of revenue, up from minimal five years ago. Legacy database licensing is declining but still throws off cash. OCI is the growth engine.

**Competitive Moat:** Oracle's multicloud strategy is unique—they embed Oracle databases directly inside AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. No competitor does this. Multicloud database revenue grew 817% YoY (small base). They have 211+ data center regions live or planned.

## AI/Cloud Positioning

**Oracle's AI Bet — The "Cloud Landlord" Strategy:**

Oracle positioned itself as infrastructure for AI training, not an AI model builder. This is the picks-and-shovels play.

**Key Partnerships:**
- **Stargate Project:** Founding partner with OpenAI and SoftBank. $300B+ committed over 5 years for 4.5 gigawatts of AI compute capacity. Largest cloud contract in history.
- **OpenAI:** Direct $30B cloud services agreement signed June 2025. OCI provides training infrastructure alongside Microsoft Azure.
- **xAI, Meta, NVIDIA:** All signed OCI capacity commitments.

**Why OCI Wins AI Deals:**
1. RDMA networking = faster, cheaper AI training than AWS/Azure
2. Chip neutrality (runs NVIDIA + AMD, no proprietary silicon)
3. Lower price point vs hyperscalers
4. Larry Ellison's relationships (Trump administration access helps)

**Market Position:** OCI market share remains single digits vs AWS (~32%), Azure (~23%), and GCP (~11%). But growth rate (68%) far exceeds rivals, and AI workloads are the fastest-growing segment.

## Bull Case

1. **$523B Backlog Is Real:** Non-cancellable contracts from Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI. 8+ years of current revenue committed. Short-term RPO accelerating (+40% YoY).

2. **OCI Is the AI Landlord:** 68% growth outpaces every hyperscaler. OpenAI/Stargate validates the infrastructure. Oracle becomes the fourth major cloud provider.

3. **Valuation Reset:** P/E compressed from 50x+ to ~25-30x forward on FY27 estimates. If execution continues, multiple re-rates.

4. **Larry Ellison Track Record:** 47 years of defying doubters. Oracle survived every tech cycle. Ellison's personal urgency on AI is clear.

5. **Multicloud Moat:** No competitor embeds databases across all three rival clouds. Sticky enterprise relationships.

---

## Bear Case

1. **Debt Is Dangerous:** $100B+ debt, negative FCF, off-balance-sheet lease obligations approaching $250B. If demand softens or capex overruns, balance sheet stress escalates.

2. **Execution Risk Is Massive:** Converting $523B backlog requires building and operating data centers that don't exist yet. Five-year execution timeline. No room for error.

3. **Stargate Questions:** Project has minimal staff, no direct data center control. Partner disputes have caused delays. Headline numbers may overstate Oracle's actual economics.

4. **Legacy Decline:** License revenue declining 3% YoY. Margin compression during cloud transition. Classic innovator's dilemma.

5. **Single-Person Risk:** Larry Ellison is 81. Oracle's strategy and culture are deeply tied to him. New CEO (Mike Sicilia) is unproven.

6. **Legal Overhang:** Securities fraud lawsuit creates uncertainty through April 2026.
